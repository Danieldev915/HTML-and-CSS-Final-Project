Chuchu

  • A gelatinous creature.
  • Likes to hide in trees and the ground.
  • Drops: Chuchu jelly

Keese

  • One-eyed monster that travels in groups.
  • Prefers dark spaces.
  • Drops: Keese eyeball, Keese wing

Octorok

  • Uses camoflauge to hide in various areas.
  • Attacks from afar.
  • Drops: Octo balloon, Octorok eyeball, Octorok tentacle

Wizzrobe

  • Magical trickster.
  • Can be eliminated instantly by using its' elemental opposite.
  • Drops: nothing

Like Like

  • Only seen in caves.
  • Reveals its' weakness when it's about to strike.
  • Drops: Like like stone, 5 arrows, Chest

Yiga Soldier

  • A member of an evil organization
  • Drops: Mighty bananas, Sundelion, Green rupee, Blue rupee, Purple rupee, Red rupee

Evermean

  • Disguises itself as a tree to surprise prey.
  • Can be chopped down like any ol' tree.
  • Drops:Energetic Rhino Beetle, Bladed Rhino Beetle, Hightail Lizard, Rugged Rhino Beetle, Acorn, Chickaloo Tree Nut, Golden Apple, Monster Extract, Sturdy Thick Stick

Aerocuda

  • Likes to steal things.
  • Can carry bokoblins and lift them in the air.
  • Drops: Aerocuda eyeball, Aerocuda wing

Gibdo

  • Undead creature that's buglike.
  • Very resistant against damage unless attacked with fire or electricity.
  • Drops: Gibdo guts, Gibdo bone

Bokoblin

  • Common monster across hyrule.
  • Hunts in groups.
  • Drops: Bokoblin horn, Bokoblin fang

Boss Bokoblin

  • Leader of a bokoblin group.
  • Capable of coordinating the group, giving access to new attacks.
  • Drops: Boss bokoblin horn, Boss bokoblin fang

Yiga Blademaster

  • A high-ranking member of an evil organization
  • Drops: Mighty bananas, Sundelion, Green rupee, Blue rupee, Purple rupee, Red rupee

Moblin

  • Similar to bokoblins, but bigger
  • Can throw bokoblins at people.
  • Drops: Moblin horn, Moblin fang

Lizalfos

  • Use camoflauge to get the jump on prey.
  • Capable of swimming.
  • Drops: Lizalfos horn, Lizalfos talon, Lizalfos tail

Horriblin

  • Found in caves.
  • Can climb on walls and ceilings.
  • Drops: Horriblin horn, Horriblin claw

Soldier Construct

  • Robotic defender made by the Zonai.
  • Drops: Zonai charge, Soldier construct horn
  • Avoid humans.

Captain Construct

  • Stronger construct designed by the Zonai.
  • Can fuse objects to its' weapon to make it stronger.
  • Drops: Zonai charge, Captain construct horn

Small Frox

  • A baby of a much larger creature.
  • Only found in the depths.
  • Drops: Giant brightbloom seed, Brightbloom seed, Zonaite
